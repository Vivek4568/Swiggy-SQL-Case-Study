
# 🟧 Project – Swiggy Case Study (SQL Data Analysis)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Swiggy's operational data using advanced SQL queries to extract meaningful business insights.

### 📈 Result Dataset
Generated using SQL queries including:
- Customer order summaries

- Delivery partner performance

---

## 🧠 Business Questions Solved

1. Customers living in Delhi  
2. Average restaurant rating in Mumbai  
3. Customers with at least one order  
4. Total orders per customer  
5. Total revenue per restaurant  
6. Top 5 restaurants by rating  
7. Customers with zero orders  
8. Orders per customer in Mumbai  
9. Orders in last 30 days  
10. Delivery partners with more than 1 delivery  
11. Customers ordering on exactly 3 days  
12. Delivery partner with most customers  
13. Customers from same city ordering same restaurant on different dates
